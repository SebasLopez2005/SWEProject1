# Round 2 requirements — Claude (Repwise) revision

**Student feedback, November 11, 2026, after reviewing [Claude One Shot 1](../experiments/round-01/claude/README.md) on TestFlight.** These requirements are added on top of the 31 FW stories, TR-01–13 and [UI-01–03](ROUND_02_UI.md). Nothing earlier is removed.

> "The app is too zoomed in; it doesn't feel premium. For the body diagram, use an actual body, not shapes of the muscles, with a way of selecting male or female, and a way of seeing how to perform an exercise — a video or text of the correct form. The app feels laggy, not as responsive as I would like."

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UI-04 | **Premium, iOS-native density.** Use a compact type scale close to iOS defaults (body about 15–16 pt, secondary 13 pt, captions 11–12 pt), large-title navigation headers, the native iOS tab bar (Liquid Glass on iOS 26), inset-grouped rows with hairline separators instead of oversized cards, and tabular figures for numbers. Touch targets stay at least 44 pt. | At 100 % text on an iPhone 17 Pro, the Train tab shows the date, status, start actions and at least two routines without scrolling. An active workout shows at least two complete set rows per exercise card. At 200 %, every control is still reachable and nothing is clipped. |
| UI-05 | **Responsiveness.** Recording, skipping or editing a set updates its row immediately. Derived views (history, graphs, muscle map, reports) recompute only when they are visible and only when the records they read have changed. Upload acknowledgements do not redraw data screens. Typing in search never blocks input. | Recording a set during a 27-workout history does not reload finished-workout analytics or off-screen tabs. Sync completing after a set does not re-run data queries. Search keeps up with continuous typing. |
| TR-07A | **Realistic body figure.** The muscle map is an anatomical front and back human figure, not geometric shapes, with a **male / female** choice. The choice is a synced display setting and is remembered. Tapping a muscle opens its breakdown. Bands, legend, exact values and the accessible list from TR-07 are unchanged. | Switching to the female figure in Profile changes both front and back figures and persists after restart. Four bench sets still shade chest (4.0) more deeply than shoulders and triceps (2.0 each). Tapping the chest opens the same contributions list as the text row. |
| TR-06A | **Exercise form guidance.** Every exercise has a "How to perform" view: an animated demonstration that alternates the catalog's start and end position photos (when available), numbered text steps, and its primary and secondary muscles highlighted on the same body figure. Text steps work offline. Photos load on demand from the pinned catalog revision, are cached, and show a clear offline fallback. It is reachable from the library, the exercise picker, and the exercise menu during a workout. | Open "How to perform" for Barbell Bench Press: the demo alternates two photos and can be paused, the steps are listed, and the chest is highlighted as primary. With no connection, the steps and muscles still show, and the photo area explains it is unavailable offline. A custom exercise shows its own tags and a "no demonstration" note. |

## Design decisions

- **Figure source.** [`react-native-body-highlighter`](https://github.com/HichamELBSI/react-native-body-highlighter) (MIT) provides male and female anatomical SVG figures, front and back, with pressable muscle regions. Catalog muscle tags map onto its regions. Lats and middle back share the figure's upper-back region, so it is shaded by the larger of the two scores. The list keeps every tag separate.
- **Form media.** The pinned Free Exercise DB revision (Unlicense) includes two photos per record (`images`). They are referenced by pinned URL rather than bundled, following the [training draft's](TRAINING_REQUIREMENTS_DRAFT.md) note to review media provenance separately before bundling. A video licence was not available, so the start/end photo loop is the "video" form demonstration, and the text steps are the accessible equivalent.
- **Native chrome.** The native tab bar comes from `expo-router/unstable-native-tabs`. Navigation headers use iOS large titles. The dark palette from UI-01 is unchanged.
